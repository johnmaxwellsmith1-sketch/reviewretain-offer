# How to Get More Google Reviews (for Local Businesses)

**88% of customers read Google reviews before choosing a local business.** Yet most businesses never ask for reviews — and lose 5-star customers every week.

Here's the simple system that works:

## 1. Ask at the right moment
The best time to ask is right after a great experience — when the customer is happiest. A review request sent within hours of a visit converts far better than one sent days later.

## 2. Make it effortless
Don't make customers hunt for your Google listing. Send a direct link (or QR code) that takes them straight to the review box. One tap, not five.

## 3. Respond to every review
Businesses that reply to reviews get more reviews — and customers notice. A warm, specific reply (not a template) builds trust and invites repeat business.

## 4. Handle the bad ones fast
An unanswered negative review costs you customers. Respond quickly, apologize, and offer to make it right — publicly. It shows you care.

## 5. Automate it
The businesses with 200+ reviews didn't get there by remembering to ask. They automated it — review requests after every visit, AI-drafted replies, low-star alerts.
