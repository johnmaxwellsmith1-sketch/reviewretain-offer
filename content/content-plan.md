# ReviewRetain — Inbound Content Plan (SEO)

**Goal:** attract local businesses searching for review management → drive them to the live offer page.

## Target keywords (high-intent, low-competition)
- "how to get more Google reviews for [niche]"
- "Google review management for plumbers/dentists/salons"
- "how to respond to Google reviews"
- "review management service for local businesses"
- "why aren't my Google reviews growing"

## Content pillars (one per week)
1. **How-to** — "How to Get More Google Reviews (for [niche])" — ranks, drives traffic.
2. **Problem** — "Why Your Google Reviews Aren't Growing (and how to fix it)".
3. **Comparison** — "Review Management Tools: Podium vs. the affordable alternative".
4. **Proof** — "How 200+ Reviews Doubles a Local Business's Revenue".

## Distribution
- Publish on the offer site (add a /blog section).
- Repurpose each post into social/email (the content flywheel).

## Cadence
- 1 article/week → compounds in 2-4 months (SEO lag).
- Track rankings + traffic in the dashboard.
