Google Reviews vs Yelp: Where to Focus

You can't be everywhere. Here's where your review effort pays off most.

Google Reviews (focus here)

  • **88% of customers read Google reviews** before choosing a business.
  • Google reviews show up in search — they drive discovery.
  • They're the default for most local searches.

    • Yelp (secondary)

  • Strong in some niches (restaurants, services).
  • But Yelp's algorithm is stricter and harder to rank on.
  • Lower overall reach than Google.

    • The verdict

    **Focus on Google.** It's where customers look and where you get discovered. Yelp is a nice-to-have, not the priority.
