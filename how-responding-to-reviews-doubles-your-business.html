How Responding to Google Reviews Doubles Your Business

A single unanswered negative review can cost you customers. A warm reply can win them back — and grow your business.

Why responding matters

  • **88% of customers read reviews** before choosing a business.
  • Businesses that respond to reviews are seen as more caring and trustworthy.
  • Responding invites more reviews — it signals you're engaged.

    • The 3 rules of a great reply

    1. **Be specific.** Reference what they said ("So glad we got your leak fixed fast!"). No generic "thanks for your feedback."

    2. **Be warm.** Match their tone. A happy customer gets enthusiasm; a frustrated one gets empathy.

    3. **Handle the bad ones publicly.** Apologize, offer to make it right, and take it offline if needed. It shows you care.

    The problem: it's time-consuming

    Most business owners know they should respond — but don't have time. That's why reviews go unanswered.
