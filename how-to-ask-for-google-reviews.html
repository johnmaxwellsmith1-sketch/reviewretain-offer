How to Ask for Google Reviews (Without Being Awkward)

 

Asking for a review feels awkward — but it's the most effective thing you can do. Here's how to do it naturally.

 

The 3 best moments to ask

 

  • **Right after a great experience** — when they're happiest.
  • **In the follow-up** — a thank-you with a review link.
  • **At the end of the visit** — a QR code on the counter.

    •  

    The scripts

     

    - **In person:** "If you're happy with how this went, a quick Google review would mean the world."

    - **By text/email:** "Thanks for choosing us! We'd love a review: [link]"

     

    The rules

     

    - **Make it one tap.** The easier it is, the more reviews you get.

    - **Never buy or fake reviews** — Google penalizes it.
