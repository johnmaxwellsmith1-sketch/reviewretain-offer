How to Ask for Google Reviews (Without Being Awkward)

Asking for a review feels awkward — but it's the single most effective thing you can do. Here's how to do it naturally.

The 3 best moments to ask

1. **Right after a great experience** — when they're happiest.

2. **In the follow-up** — a thank-you message with a review link.

3. **At checkout** — a QR code on the counter.

The scripts

  • **In person:** "If you're happy with how this went, a quick Google review would mean the world to us."
  • **By text/email:** "Thanks for choosing us! If you have a moment, we'd love a review: [link]"

    • The rule

  • **Make it one tap.** The easier it is, the more reviews you get.
  • **Never buy or fake reviews** — Google penalizes it.
