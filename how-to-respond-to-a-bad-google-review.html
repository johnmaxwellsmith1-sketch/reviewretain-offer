How to Respond to a Bad Google Review (Without Losing the Customer)

A negative review feels like a punch. But handled right, it can actually win you customers.

The 3-step response

1. **Respond fast.** An unanswered negative review costs you more than a handled one. Speed shows you care.

2. **Apologize + empathize.** "I'm sorry you had that experience — that's not the standard we aim for." No excuses, no arguing.

3. **Offer to make it right — publicly.** "Please reach out and I'll make this right." It shows other readers you're accountable.

What NOT to do

  • Don't argue or blame the customer (even if they're wrong).
  • Don't use a generic "thanks for your feedback."
  • Don't ignore it.
