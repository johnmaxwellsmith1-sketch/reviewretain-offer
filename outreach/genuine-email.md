# Genuine Outreach Email (the good version)

**The principle:** sound like a real person who noticed something specific — not a sales bot. Short, warm, specific, value-first.

## The template (personalize the [brackets])

**Subject:** [Business] — quick thought on your Google reviews

**Body:**

Hi [Name],

I was looking at [Business]'s Google reviews the other day — you've got [X] reviews at [rating]★, which is solid. But I noticed [specific observation, e.g., "a few happy customers never got a reply" or "you're at 10 reviews while competitors have 200+"].

Most businesses lose 5-star reviews every week just because nobody asks. It's not a service problem — it's a system problem.

I built a small tool that fixes it: it sends a review request after every job, drafts a reply you approve, and pings you when a bad review drops. Nothing fancy, just consistent.

I'd be happy to set it up free for a week so you can see it work — no obligation, cancel anytime.

Want me to?

— [Your name]

## Why this is genuine (not slop)
- **Specific** — references their actual review count/rating
- **Warm** — "solid," "nothing fancy," human tone
- **Value-first** — offers the free week, not a hard pitch
- **Short** — easy to read, easy to reply
- **Honest** — no hype, no fake urgency

## The sample-reply attachment (the proof)
Attach a 2-line sample reply to one of their real reviews — shows value instantly (the Mr-Reis trick).
