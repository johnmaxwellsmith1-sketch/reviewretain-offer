Responding to reviews is the highest-leverage thing you can do. Here are templates that work.

Positive review

"Thanks so much, [Name]! We're thrilled you had a great experience. We'd love to see you again!"

Negative review

"I'm sorry you had that experience, [Name] — that's not the standard we aim for. Please reach out and I'll make it right."

Neutral review

"Thanks for the feedback, [Name]! We're always working to improve."

The rules

  • **Be specific** — reference what they said.
  • **Be warm** — match their tone.
  • **Never argue** — even with a bad review.
