Why Your Google Reviews Aren't Growing (and how to fix it)

You have great service. Happy customers. But your Google reviews are stuck. Sound familiar?

Here's why — and the fix.

The 3 reasons reviews stall

1. **You never ask.** Most customers won't leave a review unless prompted. The businesses with 200+ reviews ask — automatically, after every visit.

2. **You ask at the wrong time.** Asking days later, when the moment has passed, gets ignored. Ask within hours of a great experience.

3. **You don't respond.** Businesses that reply to reviews get more reviews. It signals you care and invites more engagement.

The fix: automate the ask

The businesses winning at reviews don't rely on memory. They use a system that:

  • Sends a review request after every visit (email or QR)
  • Makes it one tap to leave a review
  • Responds to every review (AI-drafted, you approve)
  • Alerts you to low-star reviews instantly
